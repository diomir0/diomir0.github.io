As you probably have guessed, **I'm Tim**. Since it is custom to tell people your degree and professional occupation, here you go: I am a **Life Science & Technology** graduate from EPFL, and after an intense yet even more interesting Master's thesis at EPFL's Cognitive Neuroscience Lab, I became involved in **neuroscience research**, first as a PhD student working on how attentional control shapes reading skills (dropped out), then as a research assistant for a project investigating the effects of left angular gyrus tACS stimulation on patients suffering from mild cognitive impairment. In the most recent news, I joined the CIBM NeuroTuning Lab, lead by Tomas Ros. We're focusing our research on EEG denoising and neurofeedback - fancy, I know ;) \
That aside, I really like **music**, **philosophy**, and **technology**, which makes me a pedantic individual not very fun to be around of. Except if you like long, passionate conversations by a pool table, a concert scene or a river, with lots of overly (and avoidably) complicated words. I tend to be curious about lots of different things, so be prepared for rather diversified (not to say erratic) content posted here. That is to say, if I manage to find the time to write. \

Oh, almost forgot: if for whatever reason you'd like to chat (about anything), feel free to reach out!
